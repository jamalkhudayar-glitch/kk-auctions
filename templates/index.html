How It Works

Bidding with K&K is simple, online and fair.

  1. Register — free account, no deposit needed to start bidding.
  2. Browse & watch — find the lots you want and add them to your watchlist.
  3. Bid — auctions are timed. Bids in the final minutes extend the clock (soft close) so everyone gets a fair shot.
  4. Win & pick up — winners are notified right away; pickup in the Greater Toronto Area.
