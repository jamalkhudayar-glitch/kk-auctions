Sell With K&K

Where do auction lots come from? From sellers like you.

Most auction lots sold by Ontario auction houses — from Bryan's to Ritchie Bros. — don't come from the auction house itself. They come from consignors: farmers, contractors, construction companies, equipment dealers, estates, and businesses liquidating surplus or closing down. The auction house sells on their behalf, and the consignor gets paid after the auction.

Who consigns equipment?

  • Farmers — retiring, downsizing, or upgrading machinery
  • Contractors & construction companies — clearing surplus equipment
  • Equipment dealers — moving trade-ins and overstock
  • Estates & downsizers — dispersing farm, shop or household assets
  • Businesses closing or relocating — liquidating everything at once

How consigning with K&K works

  1. Contact us — call or email with details and photos of your items.
  2. Drop off or arrange pickup — bring items to our location, or for large dispersals we can handle the sale on your site.
  3. We photograph, describe and list it — professional presentation in our timed-online auction catalog.
  4. It sells in our monthly auction — your items reach bidders across Ontario.
  5. We collect payment and settle with you — a simple settlement after the sale.

Why consign with K&K?

  • No upfront fees — we work on a commission basis
  • Monthly timed-online auctions with a growing bidder base
  • We handle the photos, listings, marketing and payment collection
  • One call sells everything — from a single skid-steer bucket to a full fleet
